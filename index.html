Process

From idea to production in three steps.

No infrastructure provisioning, no Kubernetes YAML, no on-call rotation for your automation layer.

01 Connect

Link your tools in minutes

Authenticate once with OAuth. NexaFlow maps your existing services — databases, CRMs, queues, APIs — into typed, versioned connectors available to all workflows.

02 Build

Design the pipeline visually or in code

Use the drag-and-drop canvas for fast prototyping, or write declarative YAML for version-controlled, CI-deployed workflows. Both compile to the same runtime.

03 Ship

Deploy to production with a single push

Merge to main and NexaFlow auto-deploys your workflow with zero downtime. Canary releases, rollback, and environment promotion are built into the platform.
